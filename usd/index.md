: This is a little vibe-coded applet I had the robot make for me to make printable calendars for outlining my semesters. In the calendar itself, you can click on the title or an individual box to edit the contents.

## Official USD Links

[Timesheet Direct Link](https://employee.sdbor.edu/EmployeeSelfService/ssb/timeEntry?mepCode=BOR#/teApp/timesheet/dashboard/payperiod)
: You can also get to the timesheets by going to [experience.usd.edu](https://experience.usd.edu), clicking on the Employee Dashboard link, and then clicking the "Enter Time" button. For a month without admin leave (official unofficial holidays), you start the timesheet and then immediately hit "Preview" at the bottom right.

[Course Evaluations - sdbor.campuslabs.com](https://sdbor.campuslabs.com/)
: This page lets students fill out course evaluations, and lets you view the evaluations / IDEA scores. 


[Scholarly](https://app.scholarlysoftware.com/)
: This is the new system for entering all your work activities, award, workshops, etc. The old system is [Digital Measures](https://www.digitalmeasures.com/login/usd/faculty/authentication/authenticateShibboleth.do), but we're transitioning away from that starting Fall 2026.

### Travel Forms


Here's the [Knowledge-Base article for Trip Approval](https://td.usd.edu/TDClient/33/Portal/KB/Article/5107/Submitting-an-Out-of-State-Travel-Request). The [Travel Request Form](https://td.usd.edu/TDClient/33/Portal/Shared/FileOpen?AttachmentID=9517f16b-eda8-48a5-b8b6-63708ffdb708&IsInline=0&ItemComponent=26&ItemID=5107) is attached to the article.
(Some of the info you need to fill this out is found in the following links, though.)

Here's [the Knowledge-Base for Travel Reimbursement](https://td.usd.edu/TDClient/33/Portal/KB/Article/10320/Reimbursing-for-Out-of-State-Out-of-Country-Travel).
That page has links to:
- [The Highway Mileage Chart](https://td.usd.edu/TDClient/33/Portal/Shared/FileOpen?AttachmentID=5177c908-702c-4b30-9727-00a180e67700&ItemID=10320&ItemComponent=26&IsInline=0)
    - 61 miles to Sioux Falls, 65 to the Airport
    - 132 to Omaha
    - 313 to Kansas City
    - 281 to Minneapolis
- [Reimbursement Forms](https://td.usd.edu/TDClient/33/Portal/Shared/FileOpen?AttachmentID=09dea4f6-d038-49d1-89e9-455671e05310&ItemID=10320&ItemComponent=26&IsInline=0) for travel (The spreadsheets have the per-diem listed inside.)
- Travel Policy, etc.


Here's the [Fleet Vehicle Reservation System](https://sdmotorpool.agilefleet.com/fleetcommander/_Welcome.asp). This is where you book the vehicle. They also have [the vehicle rates](https://sdmotorpool.agilefleet.com/fleetcommander_net/organization/information/vehiclerates). (Currently 40 cents per mile for a sedan.)
